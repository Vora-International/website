The Market

LARGE.
RECURRING.
UNDERSERVED.

Nairobi hosts thousands of schools, offices, churches, clinics and hotels. Public health standards require all of them to maintain safe water, sanitation and hygiene. Most have fragmented, unreliable supply.

We start with the founders' network, convert customers to standing agreements, and expand by segment and geography. Tenders with NGOs and county governments add a second growth channel.

The Model

We secure orders before buying stock wherever possible, fund procurement through customer deposits and supplier credit, and keep inventory low. This protects cash flow and reduces the capital needed to grow.

Pricing Approach

Competitive pricing on everyday consumables. Project-based pricing for installations. Premium pricing for branded products and WASH checks. Volume discounts for standing agreements. Deposits on installations and branded orders.

Every risk has a response.

Risk

Cash flow pressure

Mitigation

Customer deposits, supplier credit, clear payment terms and invoice financing.

Low margins on consumables

Growth of installations, WASH checks and standing agreements with higher margins.

Supplier price increases or shortages

Two to three suppliers per product, price review clauses in contracts.

Poor-quality or counterfeit products

KEBS-certified suppliers only. Quality checks on every batch received.

Late customer payments

Deposits, credit limits, prompt payment follow-up and digital invoicing.

Reputation risk in schools

Safeguarding policy, informed consent and vetted Foundation staff and volunteers.
