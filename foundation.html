Partner With Us ↗

Facilities without education change nothing.

Many people are not taught safe water handling, proper sanitation use or good hygiene practices. Facilities are underused or misused without training and follow-up.

Girls miss school during menstruation where there is no water, no privacy, no products and no information. This is a crisis that certified equipment alone cannot solve.

The Vora Foundation is the answer. Vora International provides the infrastructure. The Foundation builds the knowledge, habits and confidence that make it work — and last.

Communities where every child grows up healthy through good water, sanitation and hygiene practices, and no girl is held back from learning.

To promote health, dignity and learning through WASH education in schools and communities, with special attention to girls and rural areas.

Six education programmes. One goal.

01

Hygiene Education

Personal hygiene, correct handwashing techniques and maintaining a clean school environment — for pupils and teachers.

02

Safe Water Education

Treating, storing and handling drinking water safely at school and at home — practical lessons with demonstrated techniques.

03

Sanitation Education

Proper toilet use, cleanliness and safe waste disposal — shifting community norms through school-based campaigns.

04

Menstrual Health Education

Lessons for girls. Awareness for boys, teachers and parents. Reducing stigma, improving attendance and keeping girls in school.

05

WASH Clubs & Hygiene Champions

Pupil-led clubs that champion good habits within schools and carry knowledge back into homes and communities.

06

Teacher & Community Training

Training teachers, parents and community health volunteers to sustain WASH education beyond Foundation-led sessions.

An independent organisation. Accountable and transparent.

01

Separate Registration

The Foundation is a properly registered, independent organisation with its own accounts, separate from Vora International Limited.

02

Independent Board

Governed by an independent board ensuring fiduciary accountability and clear separation between commercial and charitable activities.

03

Business-Backed

A share of company revenue is committed to Foundation programmes. Grants fund community work only — never ordinary business costs.
