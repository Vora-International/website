Clean habits, carried home by a generation.

Teaching clean water, safe sanitation and everyday hygiene in Kenyan schools.

Support our workSee the programmes

Learning today for healthier communities tomorrow.

A tap without a habit is a tap that stops being used. The Foundation works alongside Vora International so that every facility installed is understood, maintained and kept in daily use by the people it was built for.

4 programme areas Hygiene, safe water, sanitation and menstrual health.
Girls first where it matters most Menstrual health taught with privacy, dignity and consent.
Rural focus schools furthest from services Where facilities and teaching reach last and leave first.
Teacher led after we leave Training so schools carry the programme themselves.

Programmes

Hygiene in daily practice Practical sessions that turn handwashing, clean water and safe food handling into everyday habit.
Safe water and sanitation How to keep water safe from source to cup, and how to care for the toilets a school already has.
Menstrual health and dignity Taught with privacy and consent, so no girl misses a day of school for a reason we can solve.
School hygiene clubsPupil-led groups that keep the habit alive between our visits.
Teacher trainingSo the programme continues without us in the room.
Community sessionsBecause what children learn at school has to hold at home.
