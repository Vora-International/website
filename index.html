One partner for everything WASH.

Preventable diseases linked to unsafe water, poor sanitation and poor hygiene affect millions of Kenyans every year. Institutions carry much of that risk — and we exist to eliminate it.

Vora International supplies, installs and maintains WASH products that keep people safe. We cover all three pillars. No fragmented vendors. No stock-outs. Just reliable, certified supply.
