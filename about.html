Protecting Health
Across Kenya.

The Business

The problem
is preventable.

Unsafe water, poor sanitation and poor hygiene spread diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid, worms and respiratory infections. Children are most affected, missing school and facing long-term health consequences.

Institutions where people gather every day carry much of that risk — and the institutional WASH market is large, recurring and chronically underserved by reliable providers.

Vora International Limited was founded to close that gap: a single, reliable partner for all three WASH pillars — water, sanitation and hygiene — serving schools, offices, churches, clinics, hotels, food businesses and community organisations across Nairobi and beyond.

Vision & Mission

Vora International

01

Healthy institutions and communities across Kenya, with reliable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene.

Mission: To protect public health by providing institutions and communities with quality, certified WASH products and services, delivered reliably and backed by practical WASH expertise.

"Complete WASH for healthier lives."

Vora Foundation

02

Communities where every child grows up healthy through good water, sanitation and hygiene practices, and no girl is held back from learning.

Mission: To promote health, dignity and learning through WASH education in schools and communities, with special attention to girls and rural areas.

"Learning today for healthier communities tomorrow."
