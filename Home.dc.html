What we deal with.

Water Drinking water, dispensers, treatment, filters and storage tanks. Sanitation Toilet supplies, sanitary and waste bins, cleaning and disinfection. Hygiene Hygiene stations, soap, sanitiser, tissue and menstrual products.

Poor water, sanitation and hygiene is still one of Kenya's largest preventable health burdens.

14% CHILDREN UNDER FIVE Had diarrhoea in the two weeks before the survey. KDHS 2022.
29% BASIC SANITATION Share of Kenyans with access to a basic sanitation service. UNICEF Kenya.
KSh 27B LOST EACH YEAR Annual economic cost of poor sanitation. World Bank WSP.

How our process works

Five steps to facilities that keep working

01 Assessment
We survey your facilities on site. The first visit is free.
02 Written plan
A report with findings, costs and the order to tackle them in.
03 Supply and install
Certified products delivered, installed, tested and handed over.
04 Resupply
Standing orders on fixed days, with on-time performance reported.
05 Re-check
We return, confirm everything still works and put it in writing.

Facilities work when people know how to use them.

Vora Foundation runs hygiene and sanitation education, menstrual health education and school clubs, with attention to girls and rural schools.
