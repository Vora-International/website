Brand manual

Identity, logo rules, colour, typography, tone of voice and application for Vora International Limited, a Nairobi WASH company, and Vora Foundation, its community arm.

Brand identity

V Vora International
VISION

Healthy institutions and communities across Kenya, with reliable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene.

MISSION

To protect public health by providing institutions and communities with quality, certified water, sanitation and hygiene products and services, delivered reliably and backed by practical WASH expertise.

TAGLINE Complete WASH for healthier lives
V Vora Foundation
VISION

Communities where every child grows up healthy through good water, sanitation and hygiene practices, and no girl is held back from learning.

MISSION

To promote health, dignity and learning through WASH education in schools and communities, with special attention to girls and rural areas.

TAGLINE Learning today for healthier communities tomorrow
Reliability first A delivery window we commit to is a delivery window we keep. Everything else is downstream of that.
Proof over claim Certification documents, facility checks and quarterly impact figures. We show the paperwork rather than describe it.
Dignity always Girls and communities are shown as capable, never as cases. Consent before every photograph, without exception.
Locally built Kenyan suppliers, Kenyan crews, Kenyan institutions. Growth that stays in the counties we serve.
Personality Bright, hopeful, caring, trustworthy and health-focused. Energetic, never clinical or gloomy. We are a health company that works through water, sanitation and hygiene, not a water distributor.

USAGE RATIO
White and the light neutral hold most of every surface. Blue supports; it never leads, so the brand reads as health rather than water. Orange appears only on foundation material. Yellow is permitted as a single highlight per layout. Supporting greys: #6B7684 secondary text, #E1E7E4 hairlines.

Tone of voice

PLAIN SPECIFIC UNHURRIED ACCOUNTABLE RESPECTFUL
DO WRITE
“A full WASH check, then water, sanitation and hygiene supplies on one contract.” “Fewer sick days and fewer missed lessons, measured each term.” “KEBS-certified, delivered every second Tuesday, with batch records on request.” “Mary is in Form Two and wants to study veterinary medicine.”
DO NOT WRITE
“The very best hygiene solutions, delivered with passion and excellence!” “Kenya's leading water company.” “A portion of proceeds supports charitable causes.” “Help this poor helpless girl who has nothing.”

No superlatives, no exclamation marks, no vague percentages. Lead with health outcomes, not deliveries. Never describe the business as a water company. Names and ambitions before circumstances, written consent for every photograph of a child, and no faces shown in menstrual health contexts.

Unconfirmed figures Until the board has agreed and minuted a percentage, write “a share of revenue funds Vora Foundation.” No specific figure appears in any material before then. This follows our own value, proof over claim.
